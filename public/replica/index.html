COMMON QUESTIONS

Know before you order.

Useful answers about supply, cutting and material documents.

Do you supply stainless steel plates?

Our material supply catalogue covers non-stainless steel grades. We do not supply SS or Duplex plates. SS laser cutting remains available for customer-supplied material, subject to job feasibility.

What are your cutting capacities and tolerances?

MS CNC profile cutting capacity is 1–350 mm, with a stated cutting tolerance of 1 mm. Laser cutting capacity is MS 1–40 mm and SS 1–30 mm, with a stated tolerance of 0.5 mm. Confirm achievable tolerance for the specific thickness, geometry and drawing in your quotation.

Can I send my own material for labour cutting?

Yes. Incoming material is weighed. After cutting, burning loss is deducted and the remaining material is weighed before return. The weight reconciliation and job terms are confirmed for your order.

Are UT and NABL testing done in-house?

UT is arranged through an external provider. NABL laboratory testing is arranged according to customer requirements and the laboratory’s relevant accredited scope. Specify the test standard, acceptance level and report requirements when ordering.

Are website grade properties the same as a Mill Test Certificate?

No. The downloadable chemical and mechanical properties are published reference data. Actual heat-specific properties and delivery condition must be checked against the supplied Mill Test Certificate and agreed specification.

Can you deliver anywhere in India within two days?

All-India supply and transport support are offered. The usual two-day delivery target depends on stock, cutting workload, inspection and destination; the delivery schedule is confirmed with each quotation.

Can you help with drawings and plate straightening?

Our team can assist with drawing preparation. Post-cutting plate straightening can also be arranged, subject to the plate dimensions, thickness and job requirements.
